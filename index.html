Our Story

Built from a Simple Belief
Kolkata born. Home obsessed.

"A good sofa should feel like it has always been there."

Comfort & Co. was born in Kolkata — a city that understands the art of sitting together. We started with one question: why do most sofas look beautiful in a showroom but feel wrong at home?

Every sofa we make is designed around real life — Sunday mornings, long conversations, quiet evenings, and the occasional nap. We obsess over the details so you never have to think about them.
