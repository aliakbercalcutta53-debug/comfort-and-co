Our Story

We Started With
a Single Question

Why do most sofas look beautiful in a showroom — and feel wrong the moment you get them home? We built Comfort & Co. to answer that question properly.

A Note from Our Founder

Kolkata taught me that the best rooms are the ones people actually live in.

I grew up in a house in North Kolkata where the drawing room was always slightly too formal to sit in. The sofa was beautiful — deep wood carvings, silk upholstery, cushions that were never moved. But nobody ever really sat there. Not comfortably, anyway.

"A sofa is not decoration. It is the place where your family happens."

When I started Comfort & Co. in 2024, I had one rule: every sofa we make has to pass what I call the Sunday morning test. Can you sit on it for three hours with a cup of chai and not want to move? If yes, we sell it. If no, we go back to the drawing board.

We are a small team based in Kolkata. We work with craftsmen who have been building furniture for generations. We obsess over seat depth, cushion density, armrest height — the things you never notice until they are wrong. And we believe that a sofa that truly fits your life is worth waiting for.

Every sofa we make has a name — not a model number. Because we think a sofa that becomes part of your home deserves to be treated like it belongs there.

Arjun Mehta

Founder, Comfort & Co. — Kolkata, 2024

What We Believe

Three Things We Never Compromise On

01

Comfort Before Everything

A sofa that looks extraordinary but feels ordinary is a failure. We design for the body first, the eye second. Always.

02

Built to Last Decades

We use kiln-dried hardwood frames, eight-way hand-tied springs, and high-resilience foam. Not because it is cheaper — because it is right.

03

Honest About Everything

No hidden charges. No inflated markups. No furniture that falls apart in three years. We say what we mean and we build what we promise.
